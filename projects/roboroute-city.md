# RoboRoute City — My Future City Concept

I submitted **RoboRoute City** to the **ORISE October 2026 STEM Cities competition**. My submission was confirmed on **October 30, 2026**. I am waiting for a result.

## My idea

I designed a delivery robot concept for a future city. I thought about how robots could help with deliveries, returns, checking access to paths, and litter collection. I also considered energy, time, and cost.

My presentation includes ideas called AccessScout, RoboDock, ReturnRide, and RoboRoute. Together, they explore how robot tasks and planned routes could fit into everyday city life.

## What I have completed

I prepared and submitted a presentation explaining the concept. I have not built or tested a working city robot, so I do not have measured performance results to share.

This project gave me another way to connect my interest in robotics with problems people face. My presentation and confirmation email stay in my private project repository.
