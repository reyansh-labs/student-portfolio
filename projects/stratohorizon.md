# StratoHorizon — A Question About the Horizon

**Status: Proposal preparation for the 2026–27 NASA TechRise Student Challenge.** Our team has not been selected and has not flown this experiment.

## Our question

Could a camera looking at Earth's horizon estimate a balloon payload's sideways tilt? We want to compare that estimate with an inertial measurement unit, or IMU.

The part that interests me is what happens when the picture is not clear. Clouds, glare, or blur might make the program choose the wrong line. I want to learn how to recognize when a result should not be trusted.

## What we plan to test

We plan to mount the camera and IMU together, save pictures and sensor readings with timestamps, and compare them afterward. Ground tests at known angles would help us check alignment. We would also keep track of missing images and pictures with no visible horizon.

If the camera and IMU agree, that is useful, but it does not automatically prove that both are correct.

## What I hope to learn

This project connects my interests in robotics, computer vision, and space. I want to learn more about calibration, data logging, and explaining the limits of a measurement. We also hope to share a “Can You Find the Horizon?” activity at school.

This is a short project overview. Our full proposal, team planning, and working files remain private.
