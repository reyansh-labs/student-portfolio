# Hi, I'm Reyansh

I'm an eighth-grade student who likes robotics, math, and programming. I enjoy projects where I can build something, see what happens, and figure out what to change. I'm also interested in how computers can help us understand the world around us.

This portfolio brings together some of my projects and activities. My full working repositories are private, but the pages and files linked here are public.

## Start with these two projects

### [Building and launching a water rocket](projects/water-rocket.md)

At NC State's MSEN Summer Scholars camp, I built a water rocket with foam-board fins and weight near the top. My final launch was recorded at **77 feet**. I liked seeing the rocket fly after making decisions about its balance and stability. Next time, I want to change one thing at a time and compare the results.

### [Learning to control a robot](projects/robotics.md)

In Zebra Robotics, I worked on a self-driving vehicle project and learned about coding, CAD, and 3D printing. The example I can share here controls movement with fixed speeds, steering commands, and timed waits. It is an early motion-control stage of the project.

**Take a closer look:** [Python code](artifacts/robotics/user_main.py) · [My explanation of the code](artifacts/robotics/code-notes.md) · [Chassis model](artifacts/robotics/SDV_Chassis.stl)
