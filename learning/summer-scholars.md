# My Week at NC State

I attended **MSEN Summer Scholars — STEM on the Move** from **July 6–10, 2026**. The week connected subjects I usually think about separately, including biology, programming, physics, and environmental science.

| Day | What I did or explored |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Discussed responsible AI use, extracted DNA, and explored how elephant genetics can help conservation. |
| Tuesday | Learned about agricultural biotechnology and tested water samples using measures such as pH and turbidity. |
| Wednesday | Visited the Biomanufacturing Training and Education Center and heard about AI in industry and building energy use. |
| Thursday | Learned about rocket physics and built a water-rocket prototype. |
| Friday | Launched my rocket and presented a group conservation project at the Expo. |

## Our conservation project

My group worked on a conservation plan about sea turtles and giant pandas. We used AI to help with research and organizing ideas, and we explained its role in our presentation. We still had to decide what information made sense and communicate our ideas clearly.

I enjoyed the gallery walk because I could explain our project to visitors and hear their questions.

## My biggest takeaway

I liked having both experiments and presentations in the same week. Building the rocket gave me something to test, while the Expo made me think about how to explain an idea to someone seeing it for the first time.
